Privacy Policy — Yechalal Shop

Last updated: August 2026

Yechalal Shop ("we", "our", "the app") is a social-commerce app that lets people watch and post short videos and photos, buy and sell products, chat, follow channels, and go live. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it.

By creating an account or using Yechalal Shop, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password (stored securely/encrypted by our authentication provider — we never see or store your plain password)
  • Phone number (only if you choose to verify a phone number)
  • Age, gender, and country (collected at signup)
  • Profile photo

Content you provide

  • Photos and videos you upload or record in the app
  • Text posts, comments, and replies
  • Chat messages you send to other users, in groups, or in channels
  • Product listings (name, price, description, photos)

Transaction information

  • Order details (product, price, delivery address, phone number) when you buy or sell
  • Coin ("Aito Kef") purchase requests and the payment reference/receipt number you submit for manual verification
  • Withdrawal requests and the bank/mobile-money account details you provide for payout

Automatically collected information

  • Basic usage data (e.g. when you log in, which features you use)
  • Device/browser information needed to operate the app (e.g. for push notifications, if enabled)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To let you post, buy, sell, chat, and go live
  • To connect buyers and sellers and process orders
  • To verify your identity (email/phone verification) and protect your account
  • To send you notifications (e.g. new comment, new message, order update) if you enable them
  • To respond to support requests
  • To keep the platform safe (e.g. reviewing reports, enforcing our rules, suspending accounts that violate them)

We do not sell your personal information. We do not currently use your data for third-party advertising; if that changes in the future, this policy will be updated first.

To operate the app, certain information passes through these service providers, each under their own privacy and security practices:

These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their specific function and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

4. Data Sharing

Your public content (profile, posts, videos, product listings, comments) is visible to other users of the app, as intended for a social-commerce platform. Your private information — such as email, phone number, payment details, and chat messages — is never shared publicly, and is only shared with the third-party services listed above where necessary to run the app.

5. Data Security

All data is transmitted using encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Access to sensitive admin functions is restricted to authorized administrators only.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access and update: You can view and edit your profile information at any time in Settings.
  • Delete your account: You can permanently delete your account and associated data from Settings → Account Actions → Delete Account.
  • Notifications: You can turn push notifications on or off in Settings.
  • Language: You can choose your preferred language in Settings.

7. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data is removed from our active systems; some information may be retained where required for legal, safety, or fraud-prevention purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

Yechalal Shop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
